# JINST guidance and source record

Sources checked on 2026-09-16. Recheck official pages for current requirements. This skill is independent of JINST, SISSA Medialab, and IOP Publishing.

## Scope and article categories

[JINST scope and editorial policy](https://jinst.sissa.it/jinst/help/JINST/JINST_about.jsp) covers detectors, accelerators, associated instrumentation and experimental methods, electronics, software, modelling, and applications including medical instrumentation. Identify the concrete scope connection.

Research articles are assessed for scientific quality, originality, and relevance. Technical reports emphasize useful technical achievements; the guidance calls for performance demonstration and comparison with physical data. Do not equate limited novelty with lack of value for a technical report. Proceedings retain the journal's quality standards.

[Referee help](https://jinst.sissa.it/jinst/help/helpLoader.jsp?pgType=referee) explains category handling and revised submissions. Editors make final publication and category decisions. An author-side report can identify a possible category mismatch, but cannot convert the submission.

## Formal referee work

[JINST Guidelines for Referees](https://jinst.sissa.it/jinst/docs/referee_guidelines_JINST.pdf) limits AI assistance to refining, summarizing, or slightly correcting the referee's own report, with confidentiality, no storage or sharing, and no training on the content. The referee retains responsibility. Do not claim an environment meets those conditions without supporting information about its configuration and terms.

[IOP reviewer ethics](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/ethics-for-reviewers/) permits language improvement and translation with disclosure, but prohibits uploading manuscript material, scientific evaluation by AI, generated citations, or substantive AI-written review content. JINST-specific instructions govern its workflow; verify applicable instructions rather than assuming every general publisher provision is identical. Where applicability is unclear, provide a generic human-review checklist or help with eligible report wording while the referee resolves it with the editor.

These limits concern an assigned review. Author-side checks and ordinary critique of public research have a different purpose; do not silently relabel a referee assignment to bypass its conditions.

## Author-side work

[IOP author AI guidance](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/generative-ai-tools/) describes disclosure for AI assistance and prohibits uploading referee reports and generating substantive responses to them. Check its applicability to the task before processing review correspondence. Do not invent a JINST disclosure statement or claim editorial approval.

The journal's [official site](https://jinst.sissa.it/jinst/) links current author instructions. When checking formatting, article length, required declarations, data statements, or submission files, use the current instructions and the relevant article category. Record the source and access date for a specific compliance finding. If access fails, mark the requirement unverified rather than borrowing a rule from another journal.
